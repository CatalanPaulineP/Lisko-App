LisKo — Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Mobile Student Travel Safety & Emergency Monitoring Application
Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Process

LisKo processes information strictly necessary for student travel safety monitoring between home and campus:

  • Firebase Anonymous Account: Automatically assigned upon first app launch without requiring student name, email address, or password registration.
  • Trusted Contacts & Preferences: Names, phone numbers, home location coordinates, and commute duration presets are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • Local Trip History: Stored locally on your phone and automatically pruned after 30 days during trip history cleanup.
  • Cloud Safety Records: Trip status, emergency event logs, and time-limited verification reference IDs are stored securely in Cloud Firestore.
  • Location Coordinates: Obtained during active geofence arrival checks, Manual SOS, or unanswered safety check timeouts, and processed locally or via Android system geocoding.
  • Device Identifiers: A locally generated Installation ID (installationId) and Anonymous Auth UID isolate your cloud records without personal user accounts.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely for core safety communication and travel monitoring:

  • Managing active commute countdown timers and detecting destination geofence arrival.
  • Automatically sending Safe-Arrival SMS notifications to your Primary Emergency Contact when you confirm safety.
  • Automatically sending emergency SMS alerts containing available location information, such as a readable location description and GPS coordinates, to trusted contacts during Manual SOS or unanswered safety checks.
  • Verifying authenticated user data deletion requests.

3. Zero-Surveillance Architecture Commitment

LisKo is designed for trip-based safety monitoring rather than continuous, round-the-clock surveillance. Location monitoring is activated during student-initiated trips and may continue in the background while a trip is active. Location may also be obtained when emergency features are triggered. LisKo does not provide a public or administrator live-tracking web dashboard.

4. SMS & Network Communication Privacy

  • Emergency and safe-arrival SMS alerts are transmitted directly via your phone's cellular SIM card network.
  • Cellular SMS relies on mobile network signal availability. GPS coordinate accuracy depends on satellite visibility and device hardware state.
  • LisKo does NOT read, access, or receive SMS messages from your inbox (READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS permissions are NOT requested or used).
  • SMS alerts already transmitted to trusted contacts are stored on recipient mobile devices outside LisKo's control.
  • Cloud Firestore communication uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 channels. Cellular SMS operates over standard mobile network signaling.

5. Data Storage & Retention

  • Local Storage: Trusted contacts and app preferences remain strictly localized on your phone. Local trip history is automatically pruned on device after 30 days during trip history cleanup.
  • Cloud Firestore Storage: Production trip logs and emergency records are stored securely in Cloud Firestore to support travel safety features and are NOT automatically deleted after 30 days.

6. Data Deletion Instructions

Students can request deletion of their cloud trip logs and emergency event records at any time:

  1. Open the LisKo app on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings Tab.
  3. Tap Request Data Deletion under Data Privacy.
  4. Tap Verify My Deletion Request to generate a time-limited verification reference ID (e.g. REF-3F05B702050BEC13FC1A73CBC55A3CE0) valid for up to 24 hours.
  5. Tap Send Email Request (or copy your Reference ID) to email liskoapp.team@gmail.com. Verification references expire after 24 hours but are not automatically removed from Cloud Firestore upon expiration. Deletion requests are processed manually by project maintainers upon email receipt and verification.

Submitting a deletion request does not immediately delete cloud records. An authorized LisKo maintainer must first verify the request and manually process the applicable records through the Firebase Console.

Uninstalled App Notice: Because LisKo uses accountless Anonymous Authentication, reinstalling the app assigns a new Anonymous UID. If you uninstalled the app before generating a Reference ID, email liskoapp.team@gmail.com with general travel details (such as approximate travel dates or destination municipality) to help project maintainers locate unlinked records. Additional verification may be required.
